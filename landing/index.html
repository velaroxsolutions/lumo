How it works

One evening ritual.
Everything else orbits it.

01 · CLOSE TODAY

Tap what got done.

Everything else is a fact, not a failure. Push it to tomorrow or let it go.

02 · ONE LINE

What worked, what didn't.

One line and a tap for energy. Two taps to skip.

03 · PLAN TOMORROW

Pull from your backlog.

With a heads-up when you've planned nine hours into a six-hour day.

31

Then the dot lights.

Each day you close lights one dot of the week. The streak counts days you showed up — not perfect days — and two missed days a month are covered automatically.

Why it's different

Serious about your goals.
Fun to come back to.

One Focus Goal

Exactly one goal pinned to the top of every screen, with a bar that moves when you do the work — and a projected finish date once you've built some pace.

It learns your real pace

One tap after a task: how long did it really take? After a few weeks lumo knows.

"You estimate 30m, you take 48m."

Systems, not settings

Pick Backlog → Tomorrow, Time Blocker or Top 3 in one tap. No 200-toggle config screen. Ever.

It never punishes you

No guilt trips, no streak-loss shaming, no comparing you to other people. A bad day is easier to come back from, not harder.
